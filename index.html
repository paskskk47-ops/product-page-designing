Product Overview

In pipelines, pipe alignment clamps should be used in order to make pipe connections in the best way and in the shortest time. Technical Pipeline Fit Up Clamps hold the pipe at an angle of 120 degrees from 3 positions, allowing accurate alignment of pipe, elbow, welding neck flange, tee, reduction, cap and other fittings.

Suitable for use in open field, construction site, factory or workshop, these clamps reduce installation time, minimize errors and prevent damage to pipes and fittings.

KEY FEATURES

Manual Adjustable Pressure

The pressure force can be easily adjusted manually. No second personnel required.

0° – 360° Adjustment

Pipe alignment during installation – free to fasten your clamp between 0 and 360°.

Robust Steel Construction

Main body consists of a double-walled steel plate.

1 + 3 + 1 Adjustment

Creates precise alignment at 3 points and can be fixed with a single movement.

Suitable for Coated Pipes

Can be used on coated pipes. Recommended for pipe wall thickness between 1 mm and 8 mm.

Precision Manufacturing

Manufactured using precision laser technology and CNC technology.

Locking Nuts & Fine Adjustment

Manual locking nuts with fine adjustment screws for fast assembly.

Replaceable Block Parts

Block parts are easy to separate and replaceable.

Corrosion & Heat Resistant

All moving parts are electro galvanized against welding splash. Resistant to welding effect temperature.
