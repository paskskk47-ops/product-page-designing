ASSEMBLY WORKFLOW

How Technical Pipeline Clamps Work

01

Position at 120° Angle

Place the clamp frame around the pipe connection. The 3-position contact points naturally center the fitting at a true 120-degree angle.

02

Tighten Trapezoidal Screw

Turn the ergonomic star Bakelite handle. The electro-galvanized trapezoidal thread applies smooth, high-pressure alignment force.

03

Execute Flawless Weld

Perform root pass welding. The clamp stays firmly locked on-site without slipping, ensuring gap accuracy and zero wall distortion.
